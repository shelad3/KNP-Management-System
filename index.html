How to install

Takes less than a minute

1

Download the APK — Tap Download Latest APK above, or scan the QR code with your phone camera.

2

Open the file — Locate it in your Downloads folder and tap to install.

3

Allow installation — If prompted, enable Install from unknown sources in Android Settings > Security. Google Play Protect may ask you to confirm — tap Install anyway.

4

Launch & register — Open the app, create an account with your student email, and select your class.

5

Stay updated — New APKs show up here automatically; the app itself also checks for in-app updates on launch.
